The Lab runs events for several distinct audiences: 1Ls coming into the law school, faculty experimenting with AI in the classroom, judges and clerks participating in the Judiciary Testbed, and the broader Penn community.

## Recent and historical events

### AI Law Lab Bootcamp *(Spring 2026)*

A 2-credit, intensive 2-weekend capstone course delivered March 21–22 and 28–29, 2026. Practice-oriented, built around hands-on simulations using real AI legal tools. Two parallel tracks shared opening and closing sessions:

- **Corporate Track — AI-Powered Due Diligence Sprint.** Anchored on Hershey's 2017 acquisition of Amplify Snack Brands. Students worked in buyer and seller teams with deliberately asymmetric information, triaged 500+ contracts using AI tools, negotiated representations and warranties, and presented Red Flag Reports to a mock partner panel.
- **Litigation Track — Analytics & Predictive Strategy Lab.** Anchored on *Waymo v. Uber* (N.D. Cal. 2017). Students generated AI outcome predictions, built a Litigation Risk Matrix, prepared settlement valuations (BATNA / WATNA), and ran a mock settlement negotiation.
- **AI Ethics module.** A four-part framework spanning the personhood / attribution gap, agentic AI, physical AI and alignment, and contract liability allocation — the "AI Contract Stack."

Speaker program drew from Penn's industry partners and across the legal-AI vendor landscape.

### AI 1L Orientation *(September 2025; September 2023)*

Programming for incoming 1Ls covering AI capabilities and limits, professional-responsibility considerations, and how AI fits into the work of becoming a lawyer.

### Faculty Retreat AI Demos *(Fall 2024)*

Live demonstrations for Penn Carey Law faculty: image generation, class scripting, hypothetical creation, Virtual TA setup, and AI essay grading. The retreat materials seeded several of the Toolkit's faculty-facing resources.

### AI Tech Talk #1 *(November 2023)*

"You know ChatGPT exists… but now what?" — the Lab's first major public event, framing the practical questions that the Lab has been answering since.

### AI-Assisted Practice Exam Event *(November 2023)*

A study event for students working through practice exams in Contracts, Torts, Corporations, and Criminal Law, with structured AI-assistance protocols and post-event reflection.

### Faculty Discussion Sessions *(Fall 2023 onward)*

Smaller-group conversations among Penn Carey Law faculty about teaching with AI, AI use policies, and what the Lab is learning from its various workstreams.
