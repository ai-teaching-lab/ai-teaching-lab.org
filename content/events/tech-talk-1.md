The Lab's first major public event: *"You know ChatGPT exists… but now what?"*

Held November 2023, the Tech Talk framed the practical questions that the Lab has been answering since: where AI helps in legal work, where it fails, what to teach students about it, and how the legal profession should adapt.

The Tech Talk is the Lab's foundational public event — most subsequent programming traces back to questions raised here.
