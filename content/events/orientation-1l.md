Programming for incoming 1Ls — covering AI capabilities and limits, professional-responsibility considerations, and how AI tools fit into the work of becoming a lawyer.

Run as part of Penn Carey Law's 1L orientation in September 2025 and (in earlier form) September 2023. Builds on the Toolkit's *AI Tips for 1Ls* and *1L Guidance on ChatGPT EDU Use* materials.

## Format

Live programming during the orientation week, designed to set baseline expectations before students enter their first year of coursework. Students leave the session with concrete guidance and access to the Project's 1L-facing resources.
