Recurring smaller-group conversations among Penn Carey Law faculty — about teaching with AI, drafting AI use policies, and what the Lab is learning from its various workstreams. Run since Fall 2023 in different formats: lunches, working-group meetings, and ad-hoc sessions tied to specific Lab outputs.

Faculty Discussion Sessions are where Toolkit drafts get pressure-tested and where the questions for the next round of Lab work tend to surface.
