A workstream that uses patent law as a controlled testbed for AI-assisted legal simulation. Patent law is a useful proving ground because the doctrine is bounded, the documents are highly structured, and the questions admit relatively crisp answers — which means AI behavior is easier to evaluate against ground truth than in messier domains.

## What we're doing

- **Patent law as testbed.** Build out simulations of patent prosecution, claim drafting, and opposition workflows.
- **Open-source the tools.** Everything we build here ships as open-source — for use by other law schools, for replication, and for anyone who wants to extend the testbed to other doctrinal areas.

Scoped for summer 2026; build deferred until after Tier 2 / Tier 3 workstreams have owners and momentum.
