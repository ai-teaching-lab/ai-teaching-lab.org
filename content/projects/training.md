The Training workstream inventories AI training resources that already exist across Penn (and beyond), adapts them for legal-education audiences, and redeploys them for faculty, staff, teaching assistants, and students at Penn Carey Law.

## What we're doing

- **Inventory.** Map the AI training programs that already exist on campus — what they cover, who they're for, where the gaps are for legal audiences.
- **Adapt.** Translate generic AI training into legal-context training: the questions are different, the risks are different, the workflows are different.
- **Redeploy.** Run training sessions for faculty, staff, TAs, and students — and ship the materials so other law schools can run them too.
