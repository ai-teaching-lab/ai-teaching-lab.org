A monthly newsletter covering AI developments that matter for legal education and practice — model releases, courtroom incidents, regulatory moves, classroom experiments, tool reviews. Written for faculty, students, and the broader legal community.

## What we're doing

- **Monthly cadence.** Published since February 2025; transitioning to a Substack home for public subscription.
- **Curation pipeline.** Building automated tooling that collects candidate items through the month so the editorial pass is about *selecting* and *writing*, not hunting for material.
- **Distribution.** Email subscription via Substack; archive on this site.

## Where to find it

The Substack site is being set up. In the meantime, archived issues live in the Lab's Drive folder; back issues will migrate to the public archive once the Substack is live.
