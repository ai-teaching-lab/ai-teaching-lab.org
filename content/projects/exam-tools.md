Faculty-facing tools that use AI to help write better law-school exams: MCQ generators that hit the right Bloom's level on the right doctrine, fact-pattern generators that produce realistic facts with credible legal issues, and grading aids that surface inconsistencies across a stack of essays.

## What we're doing

- **MCQ generator.** Extending an existing prototype that drafts multiple-choice questions from course materials, with structural quality checks built in (Haladyna-Downing-Rodriguez taxonomy, single best answer, no clueing).
- **Essay / fact-pattern generator.** A drafting assistant for issue-spotter and policy-question style exam prompts.
- **Pedagogy-first.** Tools are designed for faculty workflow — not as one-shot demos. The point is something professors will actually use the night before an exam goes live.
