A workstream focused on the Lab's external presence: the discussion forums, peer networks, and channels through which the Lab participates in the broader conversation about AI in legal education and practice.

## What we're doing

- **Open-source discussion forum.** A space — likely Discord — for faculty, students, and practitioners working on the same problems to share what they're trying.
- **Newsletter pipeline.** Coordination with the Lab's monthly newsletter (workstream #11) — automated collection of items worth including, an editorial workflow that doesn't depend on one person remembering everything.
