The Project's *teach* pillar takes findings and tools out to the people who can use them: law students through course tools and virtual TAs; law faculty through assessment innovations and pedagogy resources; judges and chambers through use-case research and training; and (next phase) small and medium law firms — an underserved segment of the profession.

{{< pullquote cite="From the Prompt Guide & Scenarios" >}}
Just because the tool says something that sounds plausible doesn't mean it's right.
{{< /pullquote >}}

Direct teaching, training programs, public-facing toolkit publications, and the Project's faculty-pedagogy support function all live here. Many of our projects span multiple pillars; *teach* is where most of the Project's work eventually surfaces.
