The Lab's oldest publication and still in active use. Introduces the **CRAFTED** prompting framework with three worked-through scenarios: a Contracts class-prep workflow, Constitutional Law practice questions, and a CoCounsel research session.

CRAFTED has held up well across model generations. The framework focuses on what's stable about prompting, not what's transient.

## Status

Maintained internally for Penn Carey Law faculty and students. A natural first candidate for public release given its age and durability.
