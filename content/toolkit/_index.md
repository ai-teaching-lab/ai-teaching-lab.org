The Toolkit collects the Lab's practical, audience-specific reference documents. The current generation was developed for the Penn Carey Law community and is being prepared for broader release.

> **Status:** Resources below are maintained internally for Penn Carey Law faculty, staff, and students. Public versions are being prepared for migration to this site. In the meantime, Penn affiliates can access the current versions at [bit.ly/PennLaw_AI_Resources](https://bit.ly/PennLaw_AI_Resources).

## Core publications

The Toolkit's flagship documents — the foundation of the Lab's faculty-facing work.

### Best Practices for AI in Legal Education

The Toolkit's flagship document. Organizes student and faculty AI use cases by activity type and academic setting; covers AI training pipelines, efficacy assessment, academic standards, ethical considerations, access and equity, and a glossary.

*Latest version: August 2025. Revised regularly.*

### AI Syllabus Guide

Three blocks of ready-to-use syllabus language for faculty: (1) no written assignments, (2) limited AI use, (3) complete prohibition. Includes a real-world example from Professor Struve's seminar and addresses the eight questions students are most likely to ask about an AI policy.

*Latest version: August 2025.*

### Legal AI Tool Guide

A categorized overview of seven legal AI tool families — research, contracts, discovery, practice management, domain-specific, lawyer search, compliance — plus the major general-purpose tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). Designed for faculty trying to make sense of the field.

*Latest version: July 2025.*

### Prompt Guide & Scenarios

The Lab's oldest publication and still in active use. Introduces the **CRAFTED** prompting framework with three worked-through scenarios: a Contracts class prep workflow, Constitutional Law practice questions, and a CoCounsel research session.

*First published: August 2023.*

### Resource Menu

A landing page that links to the four core publications and serves as the Lab's faculty-facing entry point.

## Audience-specific guidance

### 1L Guidance on ChatGPT EDU Use

Written for incoming 1Ls: how LLMs work, their strengths and weaknesses, the "human-AI-human sandwich" workflow, and prompting tips. Used in 1L orientation programming.

*Latest version: September 2025.*

### AI Tips for 1Ls

Talking points for orientation sessions and 1L AI-introduction events.

### AI Resources at Penn

A directory of AI resources across the law school and the broader university — tool access, training programs, academic policies, support contacts.

## Faculty teaching resources

### Faculty Guide to AI Tools

Sign-up instructions for ChatGPT and Claude, data-privacy settings to check, and six research use cases with worked examples.

### Teaching with Generative AI — Demos

Two-level demonstration prompts from the Fall 2024 Faculty Retreat: image generation, class scripting, hypothetical creation, Virtual TA setup, and essay-grading walkthroughs.

### Creating a Virtual TA with Custom GPTs

A step-by-step guide to building a course-specific virtual teaching assistant, using the Lab's IP TA as a template.

### AI Use Policy Templates

Adapts Professor Struve's attribution and disclosure model with sample AI-use statements faculty can drop into their syllabi.
